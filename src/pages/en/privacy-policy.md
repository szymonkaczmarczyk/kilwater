For any matter regarding personal data, write to [kontakt@kilwater.com](mailto:kontakt@kilwater.com) or to ul. Przykładowa 404, 00-001 Warszawa, Poland.

## What data we collect

When you send the enquiry form, we collect:

- your full name and email address,
- your phone number, if you give it,
- information about the planned cruise: area, departure date, length of the cruise, number of people, type of cruise and chosen extras,
- the content of your message, if you add one.

If your message contains health information, for example about allergies or diet, we process it only to organise meals on board, based on your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time.

The website server also records technical data: IP address, date and time of the request and browser type. We use it to keep the site secure and to limit the number of requests sent by bots.

## Purposes and legal bases

- **Replying to your enquiry and preparing a cruise offer** - steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Booking and performing the charter contract** - performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Settlements, accounting and taxes** - a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
- **Website security, protection against abuse and establishing, pursuing and defending claims** - our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not send a newsletter and we do not use your data for marketing.

## On-site assistant

In the “Questions before the cruise” section you can ask the assistant a question. The assistant runs entirely on our server: a language model compares the meaning of your question with our base of typical questions and shows a ready answer prepared by Kilwater. If it finds none, it suggests contacting us by email.

- The content of your question is not sent to any other company or artificial intelligence provider.
- We do not store the content of questions. The server records only the type of reply (found or not) and the technical data described above, needed to limit the number of questions.
- Questions containing an email address, a phone number or a link are rejected before they are processed.
- The legal basis for processing the question is our legitimate interest, namely answering the question you ask (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- The assistant makes no decisions about you and does not create a profile of you.

Do not enter personal data in your question, especially health information. Matters that need it can be handled through the enquiry form or directly with your broker.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- access your data and receive a copy,
- rectify data that is incorrect,
- erase your data,
- restrict processing,
- data portability,
- object to processing based on our legitimate interest,
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, write to [kontakt@kilwater.com](mailto:kontakt@kilwater.com). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, Poland.

## Do you have to give us your data

Giving your data is voluntary. Without your name and email address, however, we cannot reply to your enquiry or prepare an offer.

We do not make automated decisions about you and we do not profile you.

## Cookies

The current version of the website does not use analytics or marketing cookies. Fonts are stored on our server, so your browser does not connect to Google Fonts.

If we add analytics tools in the future, we will ask for your consent through a cookie banner and update this policy.

## Data security

The website runs over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Only authorised people have access to enquiries. The form is protected against spam and has a limit on the number of enquiries.
