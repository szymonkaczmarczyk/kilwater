## Services on the website

The Service Provider offers three free services on the Website:

- making the Website content available, including information about sailing areas, cruise types, crew and extras,
- receiving cruise enquiries sent through the Form,
- answering questions about cruises through an automatic assistant in the “Questions before the cruise” section.

The assistant runs on the Service Provider's server and automatically picks an answer to the question from a base prepared by the Service Provider. Answers are for information only and may not match the question exactly. They are not an offer, a booking or binding information about the price, yacht availability or legal requirements. The assistant only answers questions about yacht charter and cruises, and when it does not know the answer, it points to email contact. The Service Provider may limit the number of questions asked within a given time.

Sending an enquiry is not the conclusion of a charter contract or a yacht booking. The Service Provider's reply with a cruise proposal is not an offer within the meaning of the Polish Civil Code unless it is expressly marked as such. The charter contract is concluded separately, on terms given to the User before it is concluded.

## Technical requirements

To use the Website you need:

- a device with internet access,
- an up-to-date web browser with JavaScript enabled,
- an active email address, if you want to send an enquiry.

## Rules of use

The User undertakes to:

- give true information in the Form,
- not send unlawful or offensive content or content that infringes the rights of others,
- not enter personal data or content unrelated to cruises in questions to the assistant,
- not take actions that disrupt the Website, including not sending enquiries automatically and in bulk.

## Conclusion and termination of the service agreement

The agreement to make content available is concluded when you open the Website and ends when you leave it.

The agreement to receive an enquiry is concluded when the Form is sent and ends when a reply is given. The User may stop further contact at any time by writing to [kontakt@kilwater.com](mailto:kontakt@kilwater.com).

## Complaints

You can submit complaints about how the Website works to [kontakt@kilwater.com](mailto:kontakt@kilwater.com) or to ul. Przykładowa 404, 00-001 Warszawa, Poland. Describe what the problem concerns and how we can contact you.

We will process the complaint within 14 days of receiving it and reply to the address it was sent from.

## Consumer rights

The services on the Website are free of charge. The Consumer's rights related to the charter contract, including the rules for any withdrawal from the contract, are set out in that contract and in the Polish Act of 30 May 2014 on consumer rights. The right to withdraw from a distance contract does not apply, among others, to contracts for leisure-related services where the contract specifies the date or period of the service (Art. 38(1)(12) of that Act).

The Consumer may use the free help of a municipal or district consumer ombudsman or the provincial inspectorate of the Trade Inspection.
